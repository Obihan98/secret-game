# Combat & Sieges (CB)

> Scope: what happens when armies meet or besiege a fortress.
> Depends on: AR1, AR2, AR3, MP3, CH3, TM2 · Feeds into: DI6, PO5, UI4

## Decisions

### CB1. Combat resolution ✅
- **No real-time tactical battles.** Battles are resolved by stat comparison, like EU5.
- Decisions that matter happen *before* the fight: army composition, commander quality, terrain, supply, morale, discipline.
- Battles should span multiple phases/days so the outcome is not a single dice roll.
- Reason: tactical play cannot work asynchronously for players who have jobs and different timezones.
- Cross-checks: CB2, CB3, CB8, TM6, AI2
