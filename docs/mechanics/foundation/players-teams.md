# Players, Hosting & Teams (PL)

> Scope: who the players are, how they get into a world, how a team runs a nation together.
> Depends on: WD1, WD2 · Feeds into: GO13, FI4, AR7, AI2, BA1, FA1, FA5

## Decisions

### PL1. Shared nations 🟡 (limits open)
- A nation is run by a **team of players**, not one person. Running a country is hard, so players invite others and give them access.
- **No hard cap on the number of players per nation** (draft: add as many as you want).
- Open consequences, now tracked as their own topics: balance of team size (BA1), roles (PL7), permissions (PL8), command conflicts (PL9), betrayal/coups (PL11), multi-accounting (FA1).
- Cross-checks: WD2, BA1, PL7, PL8, PL9, PL11, FA1, GO13

- ⬜ **PL9 Command conflicts** — two players order the same army; chain of command, locks, overrides.

- ⬜ **PL13 Defection** — switching nations mid-world; cooldowns; carrying knowledge.

- ⬜ **PL15 Team coordination tools** — internal chat, order log, council meetings, voting.
