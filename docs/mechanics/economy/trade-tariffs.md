- ⬜ **TR3 Who trades** — automatic (pops/merchants) vs player-directed contracts.
- ⬜ **TR4 ★ Tariffs** — import/export duties per good and per nation; revenue vs protection.
- ⬜ **TR5 Trade agreements** — free trade, most-favored nation, market access treaties.
- ⬜ **TR6 Embargoes** — Continental System as a real mechanic; enforcing it on allies/clients.
- ⬜ **TR7 Smuggling** — leakage through embargoes via specific ports/regions.
- ⬜ **TR8 Direct player trade** — nation-to-nation deals (goods for gold, goods for goods).
- ⬜ **TR9 Merchant shipping** — merchant fleet capacity, convoys, insurance.
- ⬜ **TR10 Colonial goods & monopolies** — sugar, coffee, cotton, tobacco, spices.
- ⬜ **TR11 Trade companies** — chartered companies (East India Company).
- ⬜ **TR12 Price shocks** — speculation, scarcity spikes from war/blockade.
